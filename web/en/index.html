    01 Welcome

    A village between mountain and lake.

    Wiesing lies at the entrance to Lake Achensee, where the Inn Valley narrows and the mountains draw closer. 568 metres above sea level, 10.39 square kilometres — and yet a world of its own.

    Walking through the village you see farmhouses, workshops, a municipal office, a church with history and people who know one another. Not a place people pass through, but a home.
